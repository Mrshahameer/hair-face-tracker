    Your plan

      Shave every 7 or 15 days?

      Both are fine. Shaving does not change how hair grows.

      7 days is the better pick for this plan. Your scalp stays clean for rolling and your photos are easier to compare.

      • Rule 1

        Shave at least 3 days before a roll. Blades leave tiny cuts. The app moves a roll if it lands too close.

      • Rule 2

        Wait 2 days after rolling before you shave.

      • Rule 3

        Shave in the morning. Skip the morning topical. Apply it at night if the scalp does not sting.

      • Rule 4

        Use a fresh blade and go with the grain. Rinse with cool water after.

      Face plan for oily, dull skin

      • Wash

        Face It charcoal wash once a day, at night. Wet face, small pea amount, light circles for 30 seconds, rinse with cool water.

        It has scrub grains. Do not press or rub hard. Mornings are a water rinse only.

      • Roll

        Once a week with the 720 head on forehead, cheeks and cheekbones. Use the 300 head beside the nose.

        Keep face heads for the face only. Never swap them with the 1200 scalp head.

      • After roll

        Nothing on the face that night. Water rinse only for 2 days. No Face It scrub until day 3.

        Keep minoxidil away from your forehead. Stay out of direct sun.

      • Skip roll

        Skip it if you have active pimples, cuts or redness in that area. Roll around them, never over them.

      • Needle

        0.25mm or 0.5mm is right for the face. If the face head is 1.0mm or longer, do not use it on your face at home.

      • Beard

        Do not shave or trim the cheek line for 2 days before or after a face roll.

      One addition is strongly advised. An oil-free SPF 30+ sunscreen each morning. Rolled skin darkens easily in sun, and Lahore sun is strong. Turn it on in settings if you get one.

      What makes a face look fresh

      • Sleep

        7 to 8 hours. This changes dullness and eye bags faster than any product.

      • Water

        2.5 to 3 litres a day. More in hot weather.

      • Hands

        Do not touch or squeeze pimples. Change your pillow cover twice a week.

      • Food

        Cut sugary drinks and fried food for these 60 days. Eat protein at every meal.

      • Collagen

        May help skin a little over 8 to 12 weeks. It is a support, not the main driver.

      4. The week

      • MondayShave day. Oil the scalp 30 to 60 minutes before if you want. Shave, wash, no morning minoxidil. Night minoxidil if it does not sting.
      • TuesdayFace roll at night. Water only on the face Tuesday night to Thursday. No Face It for those 2 days.
      • FridayScalp roll at night. No minoxidil or Anagrow Friday night. No minoxidil Saturday morning. Everything back on Saturday evening.
      • Other daysA normal day.

      5. What to expect

      • Weeks 1 to 2No visible change. That is normal.
      • Weeks 2 to 8Extra shedding from minoxidil. Old hairs make way for new ones. Do not stop.
      • Weeks 4 to 8Face less oily and smoother, if you sleep well and roll weekly.
      • Months 3 to 4First new hair at temples and crown in photos.
      • Months 6 to 12Full result. Minoxidil and Hairfin only work while you keep using them.

      Honest note. These 60 days build the habit and start the process. Most hair results show after day 60. Keep the routine going after the app ends.

      6. Five rules

      1. Never roll the scalp within 3 days after a shave.
      2. No minoxidil for 24 hours after a scalp roll.
      3. Minoxidil only on a dry scalp. Wash your hands after.
      4. Missed a tablet? Skip it. Never take two.
      5. Photos on day 1, 30 and 60. Same light, same spot.

      7. See a doctor if

      1. Check timing. At least 3 days after a shave. No cuts, pimples or sunburn on the scalp.
      2. Clean scalp. Rinse with plain water and let it dry fully.
      3. Disinfect. Wash hands. Soak the 1200 head in 70% isopropyl alcohol for 5 to 10 minutes. Let it air dry for 1 minute.
      4. Roll by zone. Hairline, then each temple, then mid-scalp, then crown. Stretch the skin with your free hand.
      5. Four directions. 5 passes up and down, 5 side to side, 5 on each diagonal. Lift the roller at the end of each pass.
      6. Light pressure. The weight of your hand is enough. Stop a zone when it turns evenly pink. Tiny pinpoint spots are fine. Dripping blood means too hard.
      7. Clean up. Rinse the roller, soak it in alcohol again, dry it and put it in its case.
      8. Aftercare. Nothing on the scalp tonight. Minoxidil and serum restart after 24 hours. Cover your head in the sun for 2 days.

      Why skip the sides and back? Hair there resists DHT, the hormone behind male pattern loss. It rarely thins. Rolling it only adds irritation. If your sides are thinning, show a dermatologist. That is a different pattern.

      Replace the roller head after 10 to 15 uses, or sooner if it feels scratchy. Dull or bent needles tear skin.

      Evidence. In a 2013 trial, men who rolled with a 1.5mm roller once a week plus minoxidil got far more regrowth in 12 weeks than minoxidil alone. A 2024 trial in Indonesian men found the same pattern with 0.6mm needles. Minoxidil was paused for 24 hours after each session in both.

      3. Stretch and roll. Hold the skin tight with your free hand. Gentle, steady pressure. Do not roll one spot again and again.

      Maxzoom

      1 tablet · after breakfast

      • Food helps absorb vitamins A, D and E and stops nausea.
      • Take it with water. Keep tea and coffee 1 hour away. They block iron and zinc.
      • Same time every day. Missed one? Skip it. Never double.

      Nutri Collagen

      Label dose · on waking, 30 min before breakfast

      • Your label says empty stomach. Follow the label.
      • A full glass of water. Then wait 30 minutes before food.
      • Eat vitamin C foods in the day. Guava, orange, kinnow or lemon water.
      • Skin change shows in 8 to 12 weeks.

      Hairfin 1mg (finasteride)

      1 tablet · same time daily

      • Only with a dermatologist's prescription.
      • With or without food. Pair it with breakfast so you never forget.
      • Every day, not every 2 days. 1mg daily is the dose tested in trials. On gap days DHT starts coming back. Only change it if your doctor says so.
      • Missed one? Skip it. Never double.
      • Results take 3 months or more. Stopping brings the loss back within a year.
      • Keep broken tablets away from women who are or may become pregnant.

      Blood test coming up? Check both labels for biotin. Biotin can make thyroid and heart blood tests read wrong. Stop it 3 days before the test and tell the lab.

      Do not add any other hair or multivitamin tablet on top of these.

      Minoxin Plus 5% (minoxidil)

      1. Scalp and hair fully dry.
      2. Fill the dropper to the 1ml line.
      3. Drop it in small spots on the hairline, temples, mid-scalp and crown.
      4. Spread with fingertips for 30 seconds. Do not rub hard.
      5. Wash your hands right away.
      6. Let it dry. No hairdryer. No wash for 4 hours.

      Anagrow serum

      1. Once a day at bedtime, about 4 hours after the evening minoxidil.
      2. Amount as the leaflet says. Massage for 1 minute.
      3. Skip it on scalp roll night.
      4. Itchy or flaky scalp? Stop Anagrow first. Keep the minoxidil.

      Herbal hair oil (optional)

      1. Shave mornings only. A few drops, massage for 2 minutes.
      2. Leave 30 to 60 minutes, then shave and wash it off.
      3. Never on roll days, and never on top of minoxidil. Oil blocks it.

      Skip a dose when

      • Scalp is sunburned, cut or red.

      Stop rolling. See a doctor.

      • Pus, yellow crust or hot swollen skin.
      • Redness that spreads or lasts more than 2 days.
      • Fever after a roll.

      Stop minoxidil. See a doctor.

      • Chest pain or fast, pounding heartbeat.
      • Dizziness or fainting.
      • Swollen hands, feet or ankles. Sudden weight gain.

      Tell your doctor (finasteride)

      • Breast lump, pain or discharge.
      • Low mood or depression.
      • Sexual side effects that do not settle.
